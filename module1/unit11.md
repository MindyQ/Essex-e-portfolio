# Unit 11 · Future-Proofing IT Systems: Integrating Risk Management and Strategic IT Governance

> How a global e-commerce company uses ITIL governance, disaster recovery, and clear ethical responsibilities to keep its platform resilient against downtime.

## Case Report: A Global E-commerce Company

### Business context and the downtime risk
This report is about a hypothetical global e-commerce company. One major risk the company faces is system downtime and crashes. It can be caused by sudden traffic spikes, hardware failure or hacker attacks. The strategic opportunity here is that if the platform can run stably, its AI customer service and recommendation functions can attract more shoppers and raise sales.

### Using ITIL to prevent and recover from system crashes
The company uses ITIL as its IT governance framework to connect IT work with business targets. ITIL helps the firm both prevent and deal with system crashes. The team runs regular checks and stress tests ahead of time to lower crash risks. It also sets up monitoring tools to send alerts when it picks up unusual warning signs. A disaster recovery (DR) plan is built into this ITIL process. If the main system crashes, the platform will switch to backup servers automatically. Staff follow their responsibility to find out problems and restore services quickly. After everything works again, they record the whole incident. The root cause will be added to future testing plans to stop the same failure from happening again.

### Ethical and professional responsibilities during recovery
There are also ethical and professional issues to consider. When carrying out fast recovery work, the company must not put customers' private data at risk. It needs to follow data protection laws. IT workers should not skip security steps just to speed up recovery. The company should also inform customers clearly if the outage affects their orders or personal information.
