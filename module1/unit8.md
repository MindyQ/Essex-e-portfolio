## Hypothetical Case Analysis: Phishing Email Pretending to Be a Salary Raise Notice
### Scenario
At a tradingcompany, employees received a phishing emailtitled“Your2026 Salary Increase: Confirm Payroll Before Friday”. The senderdisplayedas"HR Department",butthe emailaddressshowedanexternal mailbox. The messagerequested staffs toclick an embedded link to verify bank and identity details within 48 hours. It also warned that failure to confirm would forfeit the payrise
### Threat Analysis
This incident is a typical phishing attack. The attackers used the email to lure employees and trick them into sharing sensitive information. Moreover, a virus embedded in the link could give attackers access to the firm's internal network. This creates a real risk of data leakage. Therefore, the case shows that firewalls and email filters alone cannot fully protect anorganisation'scybersecurity.
### Mitigation
A risk management framework must be built to reduce the damage caused by such threats. Cybersecurity trainingprogrammesshould raise employee awareness of cyber risks. They can teach staff never to click suspicious links and improve their ability torecognisephishing. For example, employees learn to check the true sender address before trusting a message.
Organisationsshould also protect important data such as financial records and proprietary software. They can separate databases that hold sensitive information from public ones. Besides, they should limit the number of staff who can access such data.
Multi_factorauthentication is anothermeasuresto block credential-theft attacks. In this way,a stolen password alone is no longer enoughto make a successful attack. Furthermore, network firewalls and internal segmentation should separate the customer database from general office traffic.
The organization also need to follow compliance frameworks.This is because the company collect and store employee’s personal data such asidentification, bank records,Itrequires a lawful basis for processing and clear data-handling policies. Moreover, itrequires notifying the regulator of a serious breachwithin 72 hours. Besides, ISO/IEC 27001 gives a structured set of security controls. Adopting this standard helps the firm prove that its safeguards meet an international benchmark.

### Ethical Considerations
One key ethical issue in cybersecurity is user privacy. Many companies collect vast amounts of user data, including browsing history, location, and financial details. Companies must be transparent about data collection and give users control over their information. In this way, security measures can align with legal standards while protecting user rights and digital freedoms.
The firm also owes a duty of care to its own employees. The data targeted in this case were staff bank and identity details held for payroll. A breach therefore harms workers directly, not only customers.Organisationsmust protect employee data with the samerigourthey give to client records.
Training that uses fake salary emails raises its own ethical question. Such drills deceive employees by exploiting their trust in HR and their financial pressure. Informed consent, or at least a clear debrief, should accompany any simulation. In this way staff are tested without being misled unfairly.
Moreover, a just culture should replace individual blame. Punishing staff who click a well-made fake can hide the real weakness in systems and filters. A blameless approach reviews the process instead of the person. It therefore builds stronger and more honest security than a punitive one.
