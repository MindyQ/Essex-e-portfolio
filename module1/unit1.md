# Unit 1 · What is Computing?

> How computing disciplines transform business and society — and where the challenges lie.

## Task / Assignment
A discussion task (≈300 words + academic references) asking you to pick **one computing discipline**
(AI, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Data Science, or Enterprise IT), give a real-world example of
its impact on an industry, and identify **two benefits and one challenge**.

The submitted work explored two angles:

- **AI in e-commerce** — intelligent customer service, AI search and personalised recommendations cut
  staffing costs and lift conversion, but surface **algorithmic bias** (higher-income users shown pricier
  options / fewer discounts; uneven last-mile delivery by neighbourhood).
- **AI in K-12 school operations** — a "smart dismissal system" using facial recognition for automated
  identity verification, risk alerting and workflow execution. It cuts repetitive admin work, yet raises
  **privacy and reliability** concerns (biometric data collection, brittle under messy real-world scenes).
